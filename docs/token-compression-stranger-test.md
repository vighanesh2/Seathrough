# Step 6 — Transfer, intuition, honesty

## Job of one canvas
Get them unstuck on *this* concept with a still board they can reuse.
Not: replace a course. Not: skip the math. Not: a video of “gravity magic.”

## Three gates (all required to call a canvas done)

### G1 — Sibling (transfer)
Hide the pane. Same concept, different numbers or names.
They must be able to *start* the sibling without reading the pane again.

### G2 — Intuition path (this comment)
The mapping must be a *mechanism*, not a label.
If the explanation is “gravity makes it orbit,” G2 fails.
There must be a still sequence that could be a next-step canvas
(e.g. Newton cannon: slow shot falls, faster shot falls farther,
fast enough the ground curves away).

### G3 — Honest level
If the tight ask is college orbital mechanics, the canvas may
*set up* the math. It may not pretend the picture is the course.
Say what this canvas will not do (no two-body problem, no vis-viva).

## Accuracy (from the same comment)
Wrong-but-pretty is a hard fail.
Example: elliptical orbit with the sun at the center → reject that board.
Sun at a focus, or do not draw an ellipse.

Hallucination is assumed possible. The product response is the critic
+ owned diagram gate (later), not a claim of zero errors.

## What the user sees
Never “we failed to teach.”
G1–G3 fail internally → tighten ask, loosen trim once, or keep a longer
clean pane / split to the next still canvas (cannonball board, then orbit board).

## Gold sheet columns (add these)
- Sibling prompt
- Pass looks like
- Intuition path (the mechanism stills, or “next canvas”)
- Must not claim (wrong picture or fake shortcut)
